# Texture credits

Real photographs used to make the dirt, dust, mud and snow splats on the paint (`mod/art/surfacefx/splats/*.png`, made by `tools/make_real_splats.py`).
All sources are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com, by Lennart Demes and contributors), which allows use and redistribution without credit; the credit is given anyway.
The downloads were kept outside the mod and only the colour maps were read. The splats are crops of these photographs graded toward a mud, dust or snow colour with seeded noise masks; the pictures themselves are not shipped.
