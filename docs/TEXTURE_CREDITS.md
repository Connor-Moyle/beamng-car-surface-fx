# Texture credits

Real photographs used to make the dirt, dust, mud and snow splats on the paint (`mod/art/surfacefx/splats/*.png`, `tools/make_real_splats.py`) and the micro-detail of the ground marks (the grain, pebbles and clods in the normal, colour and roughness maps of the ruts and tracks in `mod/art/surfacefx/marks/`, `tools/make_mark_textures.py --raw`).
All sources are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com, by Lennart Demes and contributors), which allows use and redistribution without credit; the credit is given anyway.
The downloads were kept outside the mod and only the colour, displacement and roughness maps were read. What ships is derived from them (crops graded toward a mud, dust or snow colour with seeded noise masks, and height and roughness detail laid over the procedural marks); the photographs themselves are not shipped.
